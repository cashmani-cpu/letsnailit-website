● Live 3-Hour Murder Mystery Game · Aga Abbas Ali Road, Bangalore · From ₹999/Person

Bangalore has no shortage of restaurants. If you want something genuinely memorable — the kind of night you're still talking about at your next dinner party — you need something with intrigue.

Why a Murder Mystery Makes a Great Date

You arrive, you're handed a character with secrets, and a murder mystery unfolds in real time. You interrogate fellow guests, bluff, form alliances, and try to figure out who the killer is — together. It's a genuinely revealing way to spend an evening with someone: how they handle pressure, whether they can keep a straight face, whether they'll rat you out under interrogation.

Public sessions are designed for individuals, couples, and small groups to join a shared table of strangers — so it works whether it's a first date or your fiftieth.

What to Expect

  • 8 suspects, 1 killer — everyone at the table is a suspect, including you.
  • About 3 hours of intrigue — from the character briefing to the final reveal.
  • No acting experience required — you receive a character card with backstory, secrets and goals. How you play it is up to you.
  • BYO food and drinks — we don't serve food or alcohol, so bring your own along for the evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I come as a couple, or do I need a bigger group?

Both work. Public sessions are designed for individuals, couples, and small groups to join a shared table of strangers.

Do we need to act or perform?

Not at all. You'll receive a character card with your backstory, secrets, and goals. Whether you're a quiet strategist or a loud accuser, the game works either way.

How much does it cost?

Tickets from ₹999 per person for the full mystery experience.

Where is it held?

Our private space on Aga Abbas Ali Road, Bangalore — an intimate, curated setting built for intrigue.
