Forget trust falls and awkward icebreakers. A murder mystery requires real collaboration, lateral thinking and communication under pressure — which is exactly what makes it work as a team building format.

Why It Works for Corporate Teams

Unlike passive workshops, a murder mystery requires everyone to participate — there's no back row to hide in. Each guest is handed a character with secrets, private objectives, and a stake in the outcome. Reading the room, building alliances and thinking on your feet turn out to be the exact skills that make people better colleagues.

We regularly host corporate teams of 8–40 people across Bangalore and Bengaluru, with custom scenarios built around your company culture.

How It Works

  • The Arrival — every guest receives a sealed character dossier with a background, secrets and private objectives.
  • The Discovery — a murder is announced and physical clues are distributed. Each guest holds one piece of the puzzle.
  • The Interrogation — structured rounds of open questioning. Guests bluff, bargain and form alliances to get to the truth.
  • The Verdict — every guest submits a sealed accusation with their reasoning, the killer is unmasked, and one sharp detective is crowned.

At Your Venue, or Ours

Our team handles the plot, characters, props and game mechanics — you just invite the guests. We come to your office, venue or offsite anywhere in Bangalore, or you can book our private space on Aga Abbas Ali Road. Larger groups can be split across multiple tables running interconnected storylines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a murder mystery good for corporate team building?

Yes — it's one of the most effective team building formats we've seen. Murder mystery requires active collaboration, communication, and deduction under pressure. We regularly host corporate groups from 8 to 40 people across Bangalore and Bengaluru.

Do employees need to act or perform?

Not at all. Everyone receives a character card with a backstory, secrets and goals. How they play it is entirely up to them — quiet strategists and loud accusers both fit right in.

How much does it cost?

Private events start from ₹799 per person, minimum 8 guests. Final pricing depends on group size, venue and any custom scenario requests — request a quote below for exact numbers.

Do you serve food or drinks?

We don't serve food or alcohol — your booking covers the full interactive game. You're welcome to arrange your own catering separately for the venue.
